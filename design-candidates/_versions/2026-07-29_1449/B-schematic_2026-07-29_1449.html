DWG-02 · Python / Flask
FIG. 2 — Generation pipeline with the fabrication guard on the return path.

A job-search platform that tailors resumes without inventing a fact. It reads a posting, scores keyword coverage, shows the gaps, drafts a letter, and tracks the result.

The constraint is the design: AI tailoring tools drift into fabrication, so generation is separated from validation and nothing reaches output unchecked.

  • 1Keyword coverage scored against the posting, with the gaps named.
  • 2Generation, matching and tracking are independent — one can fail alone.
  • 3Every generated claim validated against a verified base before output.
